THE VOICE FROM ROOM 9

The building had only eight rooms.

At least, that was what the official records said.

The old building had been abandoned for almost twelve years. Windows were broken, walls were covered in dust, and the electricity had been disconnected long ago.

But every night, exactly at 3:09 AM, someone heard a voice coming from the third floor.

The voice always said the same thing.

A recording recovered from outside the building contained only one sentence. "Don't open Room 9."

Nobody knew where the voice came from.

There was no Room 9 on the building's original floor plan.

Investigators searched the third floor several times. They found eight doors.

Nothing more.

Then one investigator noticed something strange.

Between Room 8 and the end of the hallway, there was a small section of wall that looked newer than the rest.

Behind it was a door.

No number.

No handle.

Just a tiny metal plate.

9

[ AUDIO LOG — 03:09 AM ]

Static...
footsteps...
breathing...

Then a voice.

The investigator's name was heard from behind the door.

He never told anyone what he heard.

He simply wrote one sentence in his notebook:

THE VOICE KNEW MY NAME. AND IT SOUNDED LIKE ME.

FINAL ENTRY

The investigation was officially closed three days later.

The building was sealed. The third floor was destroyed. Every record mentioning Room 9 was removed.

But six months later, a new recording appeared inside the police evidence archive.

Nobody knew who recorded it.

The timestamp was 03:09 AM.

"THERE ARE NINE ROOMS."
