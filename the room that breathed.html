CASE #013

THE ROOM THAT BREATHED

CLASSIFIED HORROR FILE

⚠ WARNING

Is file ko public release ke liye approve nahi kiya gaya. Complete recording available nahi hai.

THE BEGINNING

Ye incident 17 October, 2019 ka hai.

Dead Valley ke bahar ek purana ghar tha jo almost 30 saal se abandoned pada tha.

Local log us ghar ke paas bhi nahi jaate the. Sab bas ek hi baat bolte the...

"Ghar me ek room hai... uske andar mat jaana."

Kisi ko nahi pata tha ki woh room exactly kaha tha.

House ke andar 7 rooms the. Lekin purane documents me 8 rooms likhe hue the.

THE ROOM

Ek din Arav naam ka ladka apne 2 friends ke saath us ghar me gaya.

Unka plan simple tha... bas house explore karna aur kuch photos lena.

Starting me sab normal tha. Dust... broken furniture... purani paintings... aur ek ajeeb silence.

Phir Arav ko hallway ke end me ek door mila.

Door bilkul naya lag raha tha. Baaki pura ghar old tha... lekin ye door almost perfect condition me tha.

ROOM 08

THE FIRST SOUND

Jaise hi Arav door ke paas gaya... andar se ek halki si awaaz aayi.

THUMP...

THUMP...

Arav ne door par haath rakha. Door halka sa warm tha.

Ghar pura ice-cold tha. Sirf ye door... warm tha.

03:17 AM
ROOM 08 INHALE... EXHALE...

THE WALLS

Room ki walls bhi move karne lagi.

Inhale... walls thodi andar gayi.

Exhale... walls bahar aa gayi.

Room literally... breathing kar raha tha.

"DON'T LET IT KNOW YOU'RE AFRAID."

THE VOICE

Andhere me ek awaaz aayi. Bahut halki... almost whisper jaisi.

"Arav..."

Arav ne peeche dekha. Koi nahi tha.

Phir wahi awaaz...

"Arav..."

Is baar bilkul uske peeche se.

THE RECORDING

Arav ke friends ne uski screams suni. Dono Room 08 ke paas aaye.

Lekin door completely normal tha.

Room empty tha. Arav andar nahi tha.

Sirf uska phone floor par pada tha.

"Ye room... alive hai..."

"Ab tumhari baari hai."

AFTER THAT

Police ne poora house search kiya. Arav kabhi nahi mila.

Lekin investigation ke time ek strange cheez mili.

Police ke original map me house me 7 rooms the.

Room 08 exist hi nahi karta tha.

ROOM 08 WAS NEVER THERE.

FINAL NOTE

House ko demolish karne ka order diya gaya.

Lekin demolition team jab next morning waha pahunchi... house ke andar 8 rooms the.

Aur Room 08 ke door par number change ho chuka tha.
