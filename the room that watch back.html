THE ROOM

THAT WATCHED BACK

Ek aisa room jisme na koi window thi, na camera aur na hi mirror. Phir bhi... room ke andar jo bhi hota tha, wo sab kuch dekhta tha.

⚠ AGAR PADH RAHE HO TO PICHE MAT DEKHNA
CASE FILE // 013

SAB KUCH NORMAL THA...

Ye building almost 11 saal se abandoned thi.

Kisi ko nahi pata tha ki ye building asal mein kiski thi.

Na koi proper record tha, na photos, aur na hi iska exact address.

Lekin building ke andar ek room tha jo sabse strange tha.

"Room bilkul empty tha... Lekin camera mein kisi insaan ki shadow investigator ke bilkul peeche dikh rahi thi."

Problem ye thi... investigator room mein bilkul akela gaya tha.

INCIDENT LOG // 03:17 AM

Raat ke exactly 03:17 AM par investigator room ke andar gaya.

Room mein sirf ek chair thi, ek purana bulb tha, aur chaar completely empty walls.

Usne camera floor par rakh diya.

Pehle 17 minutes tak kuch bhi nahi hua.

Phir...

chair apne aap move hui.

Lekin chair par koi baitha nahi tha.

Investigator chair ko dekh raha tha...

Lekin camera kuch aur record kar raha tha.

Uske bilkul peeche koi khada tha.

Aur sabse creepy baat? Usse kuch bhi pata nahi chala.

RECOVERED AUDIO

SECOND RECORDING

03:31 AM par investigator achanak bolna band kar deta hai.

Lagbhag 30 seconds tak recording mein sirf silence tha.

Phir uski awaaz bahut dheere whisper karte hue record hui.

"Ye room... saans kyun le raha hai?"

Uske turant baad light band ho gayi.

Kuch seconds baad light wapas aayi...

Lekin chair ab camera ke bilkul saamne thi.

Aur wall par ek message likha hua tha.

TUM ROOM KO NAHI DEKH RAHE HO.

ROOM TUMHE DEKH RAHA HAI.
FINAL ENTRY

FILE CORRUPTED

Investigator us raat ke baad kabhi wapas nahi aaya.

Agle din poori building search ki gayi.

Room completely empty tha.

Na camera mila. Na chair. Na wall par koi message.

Lekin ek cheez mili...

EK NAYA DOOR.
