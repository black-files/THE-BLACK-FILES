The Last Passenger

⚠ DO NOT TAKE THE LAST TRAIN.

11:48 PM

Raat ka time tha. Station almost completely empty tha.

Arjun apni last train ka wait kar raha tha. Platform par sirf kuch dim lights jal rahi thi.

Announcement hua...

"TRAIN NUMBER 17 IS NOW ARRIVING..."

Train platform par aayi. Lekin ajeeb baat ye thi...

Train ke andar ek bhi passenger nahi tha.

Arjun ne socha shayad train empty hai. Woh last compartment mein chadh gaya.

12:03 AM

Train chal padi.

Kuch minutes baad Arjun ko laga ki koi uske saamne wali seat par baitha hai.

Usne dheere se dekha...

Ek aadmi black coat mein baitha tha.

Aadmi bilkul still tha. Na woh move kar raha tha... na hi bahar dekh raha tha.

Arjun ne ignore kar diya.

Phir train achanak ruk gayi.

12:17 AM

Arjun ne window se bahar dekha.

Bahar koi station nahi tha. Sirf darkness.

Jab usne wapas saamne dekha...

Woh aadmi gayab tha.

Seat par sirf ek folded paper pada tha.

"YOU ARE NOT THE LAST PASSENGER."

Arjun ne turant train ka door kholne ki koshish ki. Door open nahi hua.

Phir uske peeche se ek awaaz aayi...

"NEXT STATION..."

"IS YOU."

THE END?

Agli subah railway staff ko ek empty train mili.

Train completely normal thi.

Lekin last compartment ki ek seat par... ek ticket pada tha.

Ticket par passenger ka naam nahi tha.
