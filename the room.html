THE ROOM

SOME DOORS SHOULD NEVER BE OPENED.

CASE: #002

STATUS: UNSOLVED

LOCATION: DEAD VALLEY

INCIDENT TIME: 3:17 AM

CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

INCIDENT REPORT

NIGHT 01 — 2:13 AM

Dead Valley ke ek purane residential block mein rehne wale Kabir ne corridor ke end par ek dark wooden door dekha.

Us door par koi apartment number nahi tha. Sirf ek metal plate lagi thi.

ROOM

Kabir ne caretaker ko call kiya. Kuch seconds tak silence raha. Phir ek hi sentence sunai diya.

"Us door ko mat kholna."

NIGHT 02 — 1:47 AM

Agli raat Kabir ne wahi door dobara dekha. Is baar door ke neeche se halki roshni aa rahi thi.

Andar se kisi cheez ko move karne ki awaaz aa rahi thi.

Knock. Knock. Knock.

Kabir ne door par knock kiya. Kuch seconds tak silence raha. Phir andar se bilkul wahi teen knocks aaye.

NIGHT 03 — 3:17 AM

Security camera ne Kabir ko corridor ke end tak jaate hue record kiya.

Woh mysterious door ke saamne ruk gaya.

Footage mein Kabir ne door khola...

Aur andar chala gaya.

Uske baad CCTV signal exactly 11 minutes ke liye disappear ho gaya.

11 MINUTES LATER

Camera wapas activate hua.

Kabir corridor mein akela khada tha.

Lekin uske saamne...

KOI DOOR NAHI THA.

Sirf ek blank wall thi.

THE PHOTOGRAPH

Investigators ko Kabir ke phone mein ek photograph mili.

Photograph mein ek purana bedroom tha. Ek bed. Ek chair. Aur wall par ek old clock.

Clock par time tha:

3:17

Wall par ek family photograph bhi lagi thi.

Photograph mein chaar log the.

Aur unmein se ek...

KABIR THA.

Problem ye thi ki photograph bahut purani thi.

Kabir ne investigators ko sirf itna kaha:

"Ye meri family nahi hai."

FINAL ENTRY

Police ne Dead Valley building ko dobara search kiya.

Corridor mein sirf normal doors the.

Woh mysterious door kahin nahi tha.

Lekin wall par ek fresh scratch mila.

YOU LEFT ME INSIDE.

13

SIX MONTHS LATER

Case close hone ke six months baad police station mein ek anonymous envelope aaya.

Andar sirf ek photograph thi.

Wahi bedroom. Wahi clock. Wahi family.

Lekin is baar photograph mein...

5 PEOPLE

Aur sabse peeche Kabir khada tha.

"THE ROOM MOVES."

FILE SEALED
